CRO & landing pages

What is conversion rate optimization?

Conversion rate optimization (CRO) is the work of getting more of the visitors you already have to buy, sign up or book a call, without spending more on traffic. As an ecommerce CRO agency, Troopod audits your site, finds the changes most likely to move revenue, and ships them quickly. One recent experiment lifted revenue by 15% in two weeks.

What is a landing page conversion?

A landing page conversion happens when a visitor does the one thing the page was built for, such as buying, filling in a form or booking a demo. Your conversion rate is the share of visitors who do it. It's the number our landing page development services are built around, so every page we ship is CRO-ready from day one.

Which attributes describe a good landing page experience?

Relevant content that matches the ad or search that brought someone in, a layout that's easy to use on any device, and a page that loads fast. Add a clear offer and an obvious next step, and you have both what Google rewards and what visitors want. Matching each ad to its own landing page has delivered up to 3X conversion uplift for our clients.

What makes a good landing page?

One goal, one message and one call to action. The headline should pick up exactly where the ad or link left off, the page should show proof such as reviews or well-known customers, and it should load quickly on a phone. Anything that doesn't help the visitor decide can usually go.

How to design a landing page?

Start with the visitor: where they came from and what they need to know before acting. Write the headline and offer first, then design around them, keeping the call to action visible early and repeating it further down. Check it on mobile before desktop. If you'd rather hand it to a website design company, Troopod can turn an idea, brief or Figma file into a live page within 24 hours.

Website speed

Why is website speed important?

People leave slow pages before they ever see your offer, so every extra second costs sales. Speed also affects how Google ranks your pages and rates your landing page experience, which means a slow site gets less organic traffic and pays more for ads. That's why we track the impact of every website change, so speed doesn't quietly slip as the site grows.

How to speed up a Shopify website?

Compress your images and serve them as WebP, remove apps you no longer use (each one adds its own scripts), lazy-load anything below the fold, and keep your theme lean. Then re-test after every change, because stores tend to slow down again over time. Whether you're on Shopify or Shopify Plus, our TrooPerformance agent can do this for you and has made sites 2X faster within a day.

Website maintenance

What is website maintenance?

Website maintenance is the ongoing work that keeps a site accurate, fast and working as it should, from updating content and offers to fixing bugs and keeping an eye on performance. Troopod's website maintenance services work like an in-house team without the overhead: you send the request, and we handle the change and the release.

What does website maintenance include?

Usually copy and banner updates, offer changes, layout fixes such as spacing and alignment, image and icon updates, call-to-action tweaks and bug fixes. Good maintenance also means checking that each change doesn't slow the site down or break anything, so your team can keep shipping without worrying about what the last update did.
